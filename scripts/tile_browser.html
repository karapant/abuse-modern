About the measurements

Counts are map cells from numbered campaign levels. Foreground and background IDs are separate. A map can repeat the same tile many times, including fill outside playable areas. Thumbnails show palette colors before in-game lighting.

Archive resolution follows literal load calls reachable from abuse.lsp. Dynamic loads, the editor, alternate campaigns, and multiplayer maps are outside this report.
